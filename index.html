Featured Poetry

NIGHTFALL

By Ms. Lilian Otekenari Digbani
Original Work
Night arrives slowly, like a heavy cloud, Filling the streets with fear and silence. It sneaks in through open spaces, Bringing with it danger and pain. In the shadows, a hidden hand strikes, Taking lives without warning. The helpless cry out, But their voices fade into the darkness. I am one of them. You are too. Every night, we are hunted, Left in fear, lost without help. People have changed. Man is no longer kind. He has become wild — Like a scarecrow standing still in the night, No warmth, no life. Where do we go for safety? A corner? A tunnel? Even the smallest space feels unsafe now. I tremble when I hear footsteps. I freeze at every knock on the door. Nightfall… Nightfall… Nightfall… What is the solution to your pain? Is there a way to heal what you've broken? And if there is, how do we reach it? Oh nightfall, will peace ever return?
